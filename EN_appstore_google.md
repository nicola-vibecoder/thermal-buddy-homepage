### Thermal Buddy: What to Wear?

Made for hikers, by a hiker.

Thermal Buddy helps you remember what gear actually kept you comfortable in real conditions. Stop guessing your layering.

**How it works**

- **Record**: After each outing, log the temperature, weather, wind, and the exact gear you wore.
- **Build**: Create your personal database of what worked and what didn't in real conditions.
- **Plan**: The night before any trip, surface every past outing ranked by comfort — so you know exactly which base layer and shell combination worked last time conditions looked like this.

**Built for year-round enthusiasts**

Hiking, Trail Running, Backpacking, Mountaineering, Fastpacking — plus custom activities. Log gear by brand, name, and category. Track weather, wind, temperature in °C or °F.

**Private by design**

Your logbook lives on your device and syncs privately via iCloud. No account required. No servers. No ads. No data shared with anyone.

Available on iPhone — free download with in-app purchase to unlock unlimited logs.
