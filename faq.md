# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Information

### What is the current version of Thermal Buddy?
The current version is **Version 1.0**.

### Does Thermal Buddy support iCloud Sync?
Yes, iCloud Sync is supported in Version 1.0, allowing you to keep your data synchronized across your iOS devices.

## Usage

### How do I export my data?
You can export your thermal logs in CSV format from the settings or history screen within the App.

### Can I customize Gear Categories?
Absolutely. Thermal Buddy allows you to define and organize your own gear categories to suit your specific needs.

## Troubleshooting

### I found a bug. What should I do?
Please report any bugs by opening an issue on our [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/nicola-vibecoder/thermal-buddy/issues). Use the "Bug Report" template for faster processing.
